Old Google Icons: Customize Workspace Icons brings back the classic 2020-era look across supported Google Workspace apps, with older designs available for many icons.

Choose which classic design each app uses, including Calendar’s 31 day-specific icons, or upload your own custom icon. Decide where replacements appear: browser tabs and bookmarks, app headers, the Google apps menu, and supported Workspace side panels.

Features:
• Classic icon designs, with the 2020-era look selected by default
• Per-app and per-location controls
• Custom square PNG, JPG or WebP icons (32–2048 px, up to 5 MB), optimized to 128 × 128 px and stored on this device
• The classic multicolour Google account ring, with its own switch, on supported account surfaces
• Light and dark theme support
• Live icon updates on open pages
• Lightweight and local

The extension does not collect or transmit personal data. Custom icon images remain stored on this device.
