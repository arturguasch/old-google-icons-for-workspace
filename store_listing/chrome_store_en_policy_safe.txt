Old Google Icons for Workspace restores the classic Google Workspace icon style used in previous years.

Bring back older-style icons and favicons across supported Google apps to improve visual recognition and reduce confusion caused by newer unified designs.

Features:
• Classic-style tab icons
• Restored app launcher icons
• Dynamic calendar date icon
• Light and dark mode support
• Lightweight and fully local

Perfect for users who prefer the older Workspace appearance and want apps to be easier to recognize again.

This extension does not collect personal data.
